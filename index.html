Reading the gauge

  • Normal High Water Level (NHWL) — the dam’s full-supply level, 210.00 m.
  • Rule Curve — PAGASA’s day-by-day operating target for this time of year.
  • Critical level — 160 m, the threshold PAGASA and MWSS treat as a water-supply concern.
